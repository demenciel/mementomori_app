Account Deletion Instructions for Memento Mori App

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from the Memento Mori App, please follow the steps below. This process is irreversible and will permanently remove your data from our servers.

Steps to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Memento Mori App and log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to the Account Settings section.
  3. Select the option to Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted.

Data Deletion Details

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Personal information (e.g., email address)
  • App usage data (e.g., preferences, history)
  • Any content you have created within the app

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered. We do not retain any backups of deleted user data.

Data Retention

No data is retained after account deletion. All user data is permanently removed from our servers immediately upon account deletion.
