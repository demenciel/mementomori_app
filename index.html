How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account and all associated data, please follow the steps below:

  1. Open the app and log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to the account settings section.
  3. Select the option to delete your account.
  4. Confirm the deletion. All your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

If you have any issues or need further assistance, please contact our support team through the app or visit our website.
